01 — About

Who's building this

I'm an engineering student who likes taking an idea from a messy first sketch all the way to a fully reasoned system before writing a line of code. My Troy: Siege game concept started as handwritten notes on battle mechanics — getting it to a real design document meant stress-testing every rule for fairness, exploits, and repetition, the same rigor I try to bring to anything I build.

I'd rather hear the honest read on how something's doing than a flattering one, and I hold myself to the same standard: this site tracks where I actually am, not where I'd like to appear to be. Right now that means I'm deep in the design and planning phase on a few different projects, with the deliberate next step being to actually build them.
